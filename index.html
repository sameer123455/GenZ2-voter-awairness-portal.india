CONSTITUTION & DEMOCRACY

Constitution and Your Voting Right

India's Constitution provides the framework for elections and voter registration. Article 326 establishes elections to the House of the People and State Legislative Assemblies on the basis of adult suffrage, subject to constitutional and legal qualifications.

Constitution of India — Preamble. Source: Wikimedia Commons.
ARTICLES 325–326

The constitutional basis of voting

Article 326 — Adult Suffrage

Elections to the House of the People and to the Legislative Assembly of every State are based on adult suffrage. A citizen who is at least 18 years old may be entitled to be registered as a voter, subject to the qualifications and disqualifications provided by the Constitution and applicable law.

18

Minimum age: Adult suffrage applies from 18 years, subject to applicable law.

1

General electoral roll: Article 325 provides for one general electoral roll for each territorial constituency and bars exclusion from a special electoral roll solely on specified grounds such as religion, race, caste or sex.

✓

Registration matters: Eligible citizens need to be registered in the electoral roll to vote in an election.

!

Stay informed: Check official election information and electoral-roll details through authorised sources.

📋 Standard Required Documents

Keep scanned copies of these documents ready (max 2MB, JPG/PNG/PDF format) before you start your online application.

1. Passport Size Photo

A recent color photograph with a clear, plain white background showing your full face directly looking at the camera.

2. Proof of Age

Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Birth Certificate issued by municipal authorities, Driving License, or Class 10/12 Marksheet.

3. Proof of Residence

Aadhaar Card, Current Utility Bill (Water/Electricity/Gas), Indian Passport, Registered Rent Agreement, or Bank Passbook.
